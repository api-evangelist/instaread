What if a river were more than water flowing through a landscape? What if it could live, suffer, recover—and even have rights? In Is a River Alive?, acclaimed nature writer Robert Macfarlane follows that question across three extraordinary landscapes: Ecuador’s threatened cloud forests, India’s polluted waterways, and Canada’s wild Mutehekau Shipu.
