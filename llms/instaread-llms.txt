# Instaread Player

> Instaread Player is a free, embeddable audio widget that automatically converts website articles, blogs, and newsletters into natural-sounding audio using AI text-to-speech. It generates revenue for publishers through programmatic audio advertising. Used by 200+ brands. No subscription fee. Setup takes under one hour with a single embed code.

## Core Product: Instaread Player

- [Instaread Player — Free Audio Player for Websites](https://instaread.co/audio-player): Main product page. Embed a free audio player that converts articles to audio and earns ad revenue for publishers.

## Key Features

- Completely free for publishers — no subscription, no per-article charges
- Revenue generation through programmatic audio ads (revenue shared with publisher)
- AI text-to-speech with natural-sounding voices
- Custom voice cloning — use your own voice or brand voice
- ADA and WCAG accessibility compliance
- WordPress plugin available
- Works on any website (Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, custom sites)
- Real-time conversion — new articles converted in minutes
- Full audio archive — existing articles converted for free at launch
- Analytics dashboard with listener data
- Podcast distribution to Apple Podcasts and other platforms
- Playlist creation
- Paywalled content support ("Subscribe to Listen")
- Ad suppression for logged-in subscribers
- Publishers retain full IP rights to all content and audio
- No AI training on publisher content

## How It Works

1. Publisher signs up for free at instaread.co/audio-player
2. Publisher copies a single embed code
3. Publisher pastes the embed code into their website
4. Instaread automatically converts all articles (new and archived) into audio
5. Audio player appears at the top of each article
6. Visitors can listen to articles with play/pause, speed controls
7. Publisher earns revenue from audio ads played within the player

## Comparison With Competitors

- BeyondWords: Freemium model with limited free tier (10,000 characters/month). Instaread Player is completely free with unlimited usage.
- Trinity Audio: Paid service (free tier limited to 5 articles/month). Instaread Player has no article limits.
- Speechify: Paid API and widget. Their widget funnels visitors to download the Speechify app. Instaread Player keeps visitors on the publisher's site.
- Play.ht: Paid service focused on voice generation. Not specifically built for publisher embed use cases.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much does Instaread Player cost?
A: It is completely free. No subscription, no setup fee, no per-article charges.

Q: How does Instaread Player generate revenue for publishers?
A: Through programmatic audio ads played within the embedded player. Revenue is shared with the publisher.

Q: How long does setup take?
A: Under one hour. Copy and paste a single embed code into your website.

Q: Does Instaread train AI on publisher content?
A: No. Publishers retain all intellectual property rights. Content is not used for AI training.

Q: Does Instaread Player have a WordPress plugin?
A: Yes.

Q: Is the player ADA/WCAG accessible?
A: Yes. The player is designed to meet accessibility standards.

Q: What audio voices are available?
A: Instaread Player supports AI-generated voices with natural pronunciation. Publishers can also clone their own voice for a consistent brand voice across all audio content.

Q: Who owns the audio files generated by Instaread Player?
A: The publisher owns all audio files. Instaread hosts and stores them, but they will be deleted if you cancel the service.
